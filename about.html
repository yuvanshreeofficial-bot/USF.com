The Foundation of Our Excellence

How UNIITED SECURITY FORCE provides disciplined guarding, comprehensive protection, round-the-clock supervision, and industry-leading security solutions.

01

What security and facility services does USF provide?

USF delivers professional, high-standard security solutions across Tamil Nadu, encompassing Manned Guarding Services, Security Personnel Training, Risk Assessment & Security Audits, and Integrated Corporate Housekeeping.

02

Which sectors and industries does USF protect?

USF provides dependable security coverage and facility management across diverse sectors, including corporate offices, manufacturing & construction sites, educational institutions & schools, jewellery showrooms, shopping malls & retail centers, and government facilities.

03

How does USF ensure round-the-clock operational oversight?

We enforce rigorous 24/7 supervisory oversight, with dedicated field officers and managers conducting clockwise physical inspection rounds day and night across every deployment post. If an emergency or perimeter concern arises, our mobile response team reaches the site immediately to resolve the situation and liaise with local authorities.

04

Why do clients trust USF over other security agencies?

USF deploys smartly uniformed, police-verified, and thoroughly trained security personnel dedicated to protecting lives and property against theft, trespass, fire hazards, and unauthorized access. Backed by Ex-Servicemen and Indian Army leadership principles, USF delivers over 15 years of proven operational excellence and strict statutory compliance.
