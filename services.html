Specialized Security, Ceremonial Guarding & Facilities

From Special Guards in traditional RAJA DRESS for weddings, VIP functions, grand inaugurations, and special occasions to corporate access control, industrial perimeter defense, and comprehensive facility housekeeping, UNIITED SECURITY FORCE provides disciplined, customized coverage built around your operational environment.

Our security personnel are rigorously trained in gatehouse protocols, dignified ceremonial decorum, emergency evacuation, and loss prevention — delivering an uncompromising standard of vigilance backed by Ex-Servicemen leadership and strict statutory labor compliance.

01 / CORE DEFENSE

Manned Guarding & Physical Protection

Smartly uniformed, background-verified, and strictly disciplined guards provide visible deterrence and vigilant premises protection. Each unit is selected and calibrated to match specific client site risks.

  • Special Guards in authentic RAJA DRESS for weddings, VIP functions & major festive occasions
  • Gatehouse access control & visitor registration
  • Perimeter monitoring, fence line sweeps & patrol logs
  • Loss prevention, anti-pilferage & bag checks
  • Front-of-house ceremonial salutes & executive reception security
  • Active coordination with nearest local police stations
02 / READINESS

Security Training & Drills

Continuous tactical training and physical conditioning led by instructors with Army service experience ensure every posted officer maintains poise, alertness, and rapid crisis response.

  • Turnout, drill, punctuality & military discipline
  • Fire safety, extinguisher handling & emergency evacuation
  • Incident de-escalation, conflict handling & crowd management
  • Access control protocols, material movement & gate registers
  • First responder first-aid & disaster preparedness drills
03 / ADVISORY

Security Consulting & Risk Audits

Practical security assessments conducted on-site by experienced operations managers. We analyze existing vulnerabilities and design comprehensive standard operating procedures (SOPs).

  • Full perimeter & architectural vulnerability assessments
  • Electronic security integration (CCTV, barriers & biometric layout)
  • Staffing level recommendations & shift-rotation schedules
  • Emergency action plans (EAP) & disaster mitigation protocols
  • Independent security compliance audits & threat reports
04 / INTEGRATED FACILITY

Facility & Housekeeping Services

Professional housekeeping and sanitation solutions for clients seeking a unified partner for security and facility upkeep, delivered by trained, uniformed teams in distinctive blue aprons.

  • Corporate office, retail showroom & campus cleaning
  • High-traffic floor maintenance, buffing & sanitization
  • Restroom hygiene upkeep & consumable replenishment
  • Waste segregation, management & disposal supervision
  • Trained housekeeping supervisors ensuring impeccable standards

How A Deployment Comes Together

Four disciplined stages from first contact to guards standing active post on your premises.

STEP ONE

Site Walkthrough & Consultation

Our operations directors walk your site with you to examine access points, high-risk zones, traffic flow, and vulnerabilities.

02
STEP TWO
